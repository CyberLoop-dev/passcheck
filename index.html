    How can I verify nothing is sent?

    Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab and type a password. You will see no requests. The page also ships a Content Security Policy that tells the browser to block every outside connection, and the whole source is a few small files you can read.

    Strength results are educational estimates from a simple model, not guarantees. This tool does not check breach databases, so a password it likes may still have leaked elsewhere. For extra caution, test the pattern of a password instead of the real thing.
